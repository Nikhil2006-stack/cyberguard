Classroom Hotspot Instructions

To allow classmates to connect their phones or laptops during your presentation:

  1. Enable Mobile Hotspot: On the host Windows laptop, turn on "Mobile hotspot" in Windows Settings.
  2. Connect Classmates: Have students connect their phones/laptops to your hotspot SSID.
  3. Share Target URL: Direct classmates to enter this URL into their mobile browser:
    http://{{ host_ip }}:5000
  4. Firewall Tip: If students cannot connect, ensure Windows Defender Firewall allows Python/Flask on Private Networks.
