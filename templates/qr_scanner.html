What is "Quishing"?

QR Phishing (Quishing) occurs when adversaries overlay malicious QR stickers on physical flyers, parking meters, or campus dining tables to bypass traditional email spam filters.

CYBERGUARD inspects the embedded string safely inside an isolated memory buffer, checks for homoglyphs, IP addresses, and malicious URI schemes (javascript:, data:), and strictly prevents automatic browser redirection.
